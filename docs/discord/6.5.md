# BotOfTheSpecter Discord Bot - Version 6.5 (2026-09-19)

## Version Information

* Updated version from 6.4 to 6.5
* Fix: FreeStuff Discord posts only include games you can add to your account for $0 (free to keep)

## Bug Fixes

* **FreeStuff no longer posts free-weekend / free-play-time games**
  * FreeStuff announcements mix 100% off giveaways with temporary Steam free weekends. Discord was posting every product in the payload, so titles such as Diablo IV and Sea of Thieves went out as "free games" even though they still cost money to keep.
  * **Fix:** only post products whose FreeStuff channel type is `keep` and whose discounted price (`newValue`) is $0. Free-weekend (`timed`), Prime, Game Pass, and other play-time offers are skipped.

## Technical Details

* Changes are in `./bot/specterdiscord.py` and `./api/api.py` (same keep-forever filter when saving `/freestuff/games`).
