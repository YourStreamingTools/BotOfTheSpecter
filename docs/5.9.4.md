# Changelog - Version 5.9.4

## Version Information

* Updated version from 5.9.3 to 5.9.4

## Bug Fixes

* **Gamble payouts and totals corrected (`!gamble coinflip`, `!gamble blackjack`, `!gamble roulette`)**
  * Betting `all` now bets your whole points balance. Before, `all` was ignored and a default bet of 100 was used.
  * A win now pays out the amount you bet (even money). Before, a win paid double the bet.
  * The total shown after a game is now your saved points balance, read back after the game is recorded.
  * The broadcaster has unlimited points, so their bets are never refused or saved. Their results now say `Total points: unlimited` instead of a made-up total, and `all` uses the default bet of 100 for the broadcaster.
  * Roulette now accepts the colour and the bet in either order, including `all` (for example `!gamble roulette all red`).
  * For everyone else, betting more points than you have is still refused, and a bet of zero or less is still refused.
* **The broadcaster now has unlimited points everywhere in the bot**
  * `!points` shows `unlimited` for the broadcaster, both when they check their own points and when someone else looks them up. Before, it showed their stored balance, often 0.
  * `!slots` and `!roulette` no longer save the broadcaster's wins or losses. `!roulette` shows `Current points: unlimited` for the broadcaster.
  * `!store` purchases by the broadcaster are never refused for balance and are not charged. The purchase message shows `balance: unlimited`.
* **Kick bot (1.0.3):** `!gamble all` now bets your whole balance, and a bet of zero or less is now refused.
