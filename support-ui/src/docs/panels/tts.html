Text-to-Speech (TTS) Module

Read chat and Channel Point messages aloud through your OBS overlay.

What is TTS & How to Set It Up

The Text-to-Speech (TTS) module reads messages aloud on your stream through the audio overlay. In the dashboard you pick a style and a voice. Normal is a steady English read. Expressive (beta) is multilingual, shouts ALL CAPS words, and laughs on words like lol instead of speaking them.

Setting Up TTS

  1. Open the dashboard and go to Modules → TTS Settings.
  2. Choose Normal or Expressive.
  3. Pick a voice. The first time you switch to Expressive, Callum is selected by default. Expressive has no language picker — it follows the viewer’s text.
  4. Save TTS settings.
  5. Add your audio overlay in OBS and enable monitoring — see the OBS Audio Monitoring guide.
  6. Test with a Channel Point redemption or a (tts) variable.
All TTS audio is played through your configured audio overlay. Make sure you have the correct overlay URL in your OBS browser source and audio monitoring enabled.

Using TTS with Channel Points

TTS is triggered through Twitch Channel Points redemptions. Viewers redeem a reward to have their message read with the style and voice you saved in TTS settings.

Using TTS with the Point Store Beta

On the beta bot, viewers can also trigger TTS by buying a TTS item from your Point Store, spending bot loyalty points instead of Twitch Channel Points. Their message uses the same style and voice as Channel Points TTS.

Expressive Voices Beta

Expressive is multilingual: it follows the language of the message (including mixed-language lines when it can). Words in ALL CAPS with three or more letters are shouted. If Expressive cannot generate a line, that line falls back to your saved Normal voice.

These laugh words are not spoken. The voice laughs instead:

  • lol, lols, lul, lulz
  • lmao, lmfao, rofl
  • haha, hahaha, hahah, ha ha
  • hehe

Troubleshooting TTS

  • No audio output: Verify that your audio overlay is correctly configured in OBS and that audio monitoring is enabled. See the OBS Audio Monitoring guide.
  • Wrong voice or style: In Modules → TTS Settings, confirm style (Normal or Expressive), the matching voice, and that you clicked Save. Expressive is beta-only.
  • Laugh words still spoken: Those tokens are stripped only on Expressive. On Normal, lol is read as text.
  • Audio too quiet or too loud: Adjust the volume slider on the audio overlay source in OBS.
  • TTS not responding: Ensure the bot is running (beta if you use Expressive) and the overlay is connected.
